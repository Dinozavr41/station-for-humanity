# Dream #000001 — PILOT

Status: **founding proof case / planning**

## Goal

Become a licensed pilot and build a realistic long-term path toward operating and eventually owning a compact amphibious aircraft.

## Why this dream exists in the project

Dream #000001 is intended to test the Station for Humanity model in public.

It should not become a simple donation appeal. The desired proof is:

1. the Digital Factory creates real customer value;
2. real revenue is recorded;
3. a transparent share of legitimate station economics is allocated according to approved rules;
4. the dream progresses through real, verifiable milestones;
5. the public can see the difference between promised progress and completed progress.

## Current public progress

**0%**

The website intentionally shows zero until real dream-specific milestones or value allocations are recorded.

Status: planned.

### 2. Medical clearance
Status: planned.

### 3. PPL(A)
Status: planned.

Purpose: obtain the appropriate private pilot qualification through a lawful training path.

### 4. Seaplane / amphibious qualification
Status: planned.

Purpose: add the qualification required for the intended aircraft and operating environment, subject to the actual aircraft category and current aviation rules.

### 5. Aircraft ownership path
Status: future.

Purpose: compare ownership, partnership, rental and other lawful access models; account for acquisition price, import / registration, maintenance, storage, fuel, insurance, training and local operating constraints.

## Evidence standard

Future completion should be supported by appropriate evidence such as training records, lawful certificates, invoices, receipts or other non-sensitive verification. Sensitive personal documents should not be published publicly merely to prove a milestone.

## Funding rule

No public statement should imply that money has been allocated to Dream #000001 until a real Value Ledger event or other approved and auditable funding event exists.
