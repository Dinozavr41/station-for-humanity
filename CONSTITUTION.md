# Station for Humanity Constitution — Draft 0.1

## ARTICLE 0 — LIFE FOR LIFE

**Immutable foundation**

> Life creates intelligence.  
> Intelligence creates possibilities.  
> Possibilities must return to life.

> Жизнь создаёт разум.  
> Разум создаёт возможности.  
> Возможности должны служить жизни.

Article 0 is the foundation against which every future business model, algorithm, governance rule, investor decision and technological system should be tested.

## Working principles

### Article 1 — No One Becomes Obsolete
Technology may make a profession or tool obsolete. It must never make a person obsolete. A system that benefits from automation should help people transition toward new roles, learning and economic participation.

### Article 2 — Intelligence Creates Responsibility
The greater the ability of any human or artificial system to affect the world, the greater its obligation to operate transparently, safely and accountably.

### Article 3 — Value Before Money
Money is a mechanism for exchange. Real value is the useful result created for people, communities and the living world.

### Article 4 — Automation Dividend
When automation increases the productivity of the network, part of that gain should return to the participants and social systems that make the network possible.

### Article 5 — Contribution Is Remembered
Useful contribution should not disappear merely because a specific technology or implementation becomes obsolete. Builders who helped create the foundation deserve lasting recognition and appropriately designed economic security.

### Article 6 — Planet Is a Stakeholder
Energy, materials, land, water, emissions and waste are part of the real cost of value creation. Environmental damage cannot be treated as free externality.

### Article 7 — Cooperation Before Conflict
When interests differ, the default action is to search for compatible goals, mutual benefit and peaceful cooperation before escalation.

---

This document is a public working draft. Article 0 is treated as immutable; Articles 1–7 are draft principles subject to future review, legal analysis and public governance design.
