# Station for Humanity Constitution — Draft 0.2

## ARTICLE 0 — LIFE FOR LIFE

**Immutable foundation**

> Life creates intelligence.  
> Intelligence creates possibilities.  
> Possibilities must return to life.

> Жизнь создаёт разум.  
> Разум создаёт возможности.  
> Возможности должны служить жизни.

Article 0 is the foundation against which every future business model, algorithm, governance rule, investor decision and technological system is tested. If a decision conflicts with Article 0, the decision changes — not Article 0.

## Working principles

### Article 1 — No One Becomes Obsolete
Technology may make a profession or tool obsolete. It must never make a person obsolete. A system that benefits from automation should provide a path toward new roles, learning, income and social participation.

### Article 2 — Intelligence Creates Responsibility
The greater the ability of any human or artificial system to affect the world, the greater its obligation to operate transparently, safely and accountably.

### Article 3 — Value Before Money
Money is a mechanism for exchange. Real value is the useful result created for people, communities and the living world.

### Article 4 — Automation Dividend
When shared automation increases network productivity, part of that gain should return to participants and the social systems that make the network possible.

### Article 5 — Contribution Is Remembered
Useful contribution should not disappear merely because a specific technology or implementation becomes obsolete. Builders who created the foundation deserve lasting recognition and appropriately designed economic security.

### Article 6 — Planet Is a Stakeholder
Energy, materials, land, water, emissions and waste are part of the real cost of value creation. Environmental damage cannot be treated as a free externality.

### Article 7 — Cooperation Before Conflict
When interests differ, the default action is to search for compatible goals, mutual benefit and peaceful cooperation before escalation.

### Article 8 — No Pay-to-Earn Access
No person should have to buy a membership, recruit a downline or pay for the right to earn. Economic reward must arise from real value creation, legitimate ownership, or clearly disclosed contractual participation.

### Article 9 — Reward Follows Real Value
Income, royalties and contribution rewards should be traceable to useful work, used modules, legitimate capital, infrastructure, verified referrals or other measurable value — not endless recruitment chains.

### Article 10 — Builders Are Protected, Not Absolute Rulers
Founders and early builders may receive ownership, royalties, builder income or other long-term protection for genuine contribution. This recognition must not give any founder, investor, company or AI system unlimited power over participants.

### Article 11 — Human Appeal Is a Right
Any consequential automated decision affecting access, income, reputation, safety or participation must have a meaningful path to human review and appeal.

### Article 12 — Open Audit by Default
Material flows of money, value, public funds, rules and governance decisions should be auditable. Privacy is protected, but hidden accounting and hidden rule changes are not the default.

### Article 13 — Knowledge Must Spread Opportunity
As intelligence and automation become more capable, access to education, retraining, tools and useful knowledge should become broader rather than narrower.

### Article 14 — Social Resilience Is Infrastructure
Dream support, long-term builder protection, retraining and emergency support are not decorative charity programs. They are part of the network's infrastructure and must be designed transparently and sustainably.

### Article 15 — The Protocol Must Outlive Any One Company
Operational companies may build, sell, invest and compete. The foundational protocol, social contract and core public rules should be designed so the network cannot be permanently captured by one company, investor, government, founder or model provider.

---

## Status of this document

Only **Article 0** is currently treated as immutable.

Articles **1–15** are a public working draft. They must survive legal, economic, technical, security and ethical review before becoming binding rules of any future organization, foundation, protocol or financial system.
