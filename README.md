# Station for Humanity

**Primary domain:** https://stationforhumanity.com

**Current public version:** Alpha 0.2

## ARTICLE 0 — LIFE FOR LIFE

> Life creates intelligence.  
> Intelligence creates possibilities.  
> Possibilities must return to life.

Русская версия:

> Жизнь создаёт разум.  
> Разум создаёт возможности.  
> Возможности должны служить жизни.

Station for Humanity is a public experiment in building a future where humans, AI, machines, knowledge and physical resources create measurable value together — and increasing technological capability expands security and possibility for life instead of making people economically disposable.

## Alpha 0.2

The second public module adds the first social and economic interaction layer:

- **Join the Station** — five ways to enter the network;
- **Founding Ticket** — local prototype for expressing a need, capability, resource, contribution or dream;
- **Dream #000001 — PILOT** — first transparent proof case;
- **Value Ledger concept** — attribution and value-flow model;
- **Social Contract** — Dream Fund, Builder & Pension, Opportunity and Emergency directions;
- **Constitution Draft 0.2** — Article 0 plus fifteen working principles;
- **honest public metrics** — counters remain zero until real backend events exist.

## Core modules

1. **Digital Factory** — commercial value creation through humans + AI + automation.
2. **Join / Identity** — people, organizations and capabilities enter the network.
3. **Value Ledger** — transparent attribution of contribution, costs and rewards.
4. **Dream Commons** — matches needs with skills, resources, equipment, knowledge and money.
5. **Module Market** — reusable software, AI and production capabilities with royalties.
6. **Social Dividend** — future mechanism for returning part of shared automation productivity to people.
7. **Governance & Audit** — human appeal, transparent rules and independent oversight.
8. **Dream Station** — long-term federated global value network.
