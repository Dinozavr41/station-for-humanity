# Station for Humanity — Value Economy Draft

Status: **working design, not financial policy**.

## Objective

Create an economic loop in which useful work produces real customer value, contributors are rewarded, reusable modules can earn royalties, shared automation can support the wider community, and social mechanisms are funded without turning the system into an MLM or donation-only platform.

## Value creation event

A future ledger entry should be able to answer:

- Who requested the work?
- What useful outcome was delivered?
- What was the sale value?
- What direct costs occurred?
- Which humans contributed?
- Which software / AI / production modules contributed?
- Which infrastructure was used?
- What revenue allocation rules applied?
- What social allocation occurred?
- What evidence confirms delivery?

## Candidate allocation buckets

The website Alpha 0.2 contains an **illustrative simulator only**. The example allocation is not policy.

Potential buckets include:

- creator / executor income;
- module creator royalties;
- infrastructure and AI costs;
- platform development;
- Dream Fund;
- social / builder / reserve pools.

Exact percentages must be determined later from unit economics, law, taxation, payment costs, fraud risk and governance.

## Builder economics

The project wants to protect people who invested years in building foundational infrastructure.

Possible instruments to investigate legally and economically:

- company equity;
- profit sharing;
- module royalties;
- founder / builder contribution pools;
- phantom equity or similar contractual participation;
- pension-style network support.

Founding contribution should not disappear merely because implementation technology is later replaced.

## Referral rule

Referrals may be useful for customer acquisition or contributor onboarding, but the design must avoid an endless downline economy.

Preferred direction:

- limited referral duration;
- one level rather than recursive multilevel chains;
- reward paid from disclosed platform economics;
- reward linked to genuine customer or module value;
- no fee to join or earn.

## Tokenization

Tokenization is **not the first economic layer**.

Before any tradable token, the system should have:

1. real customers;
2. real delivered value;
3. an auditable Value Ledger;
4. a lawful operating structure;
5. clear accounting and taxation;
6. anti-fraud / AML / KYC design where required;
7. a demonstrated reason why a token improves the system rather than merely adding speculation.

Possible non-tradable primitives may appear earlier, such as contribution scores or internal accounting credits, subject to legal review.
